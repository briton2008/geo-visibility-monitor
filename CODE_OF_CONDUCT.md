# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GEO Visibility Lab a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and evidence-focused.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Protect credentials, private monitoring data and customer information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption and other conduct that makes participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [.github/SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
